written 11/4. super small edits 11/17, 11/18

sweating, panting, focused, the tennis player reaches for his phone during the changeover. his fingers instinctively flit to the welcoming pink camera, corners of his mouth turning upward as he hunches to see 3d poly animations that answer absurd questions he's never dreamed of. he watches basketball highlights, ponders over his fantasy players; watches league of legends esports, hopes his pickems are right. his mind is completely off of tennis now.

flashy editing and an excited man preaching about landing FAANG sends him back to reality. disturbed, he realizes what he picked up his phone for. the elixir of efficiency, the productivity producer, the oracle. he checks back up on the agent he left at home on his mac. furrowing his eyebrows, he swipes aggressively at the llm's long response, which seems to have been not to his satisfaction; it seems to have produced many unit tests and verification pipelines hoping to be helpful. 

"what the fuck?" he mumbles to himself. "i legit gave it the simplest instructions known to man: 'fix this.' why is it doing allat? can't it focus on one thing?" 

he looks up. his practice partner has stood up from the bench for who knows how long, but apparently for a while given the side-eye. 

"everything alright?" his racket impatiently taps his shoe.

"my bad. i just needed to check something." 

slightly embarrassed, he types a quick good-bye instruction, and half-jogs to the baseline for his service game. his water is still under the bench, untouched.
